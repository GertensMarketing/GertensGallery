A New Kind of Showroom

Where Hardscape Meets Artistry

Gertens Gallery showcases a curated selection of natural stone, porcelain, and concrete products designed for exceptional landscapes.

Inspired by the world's greatest artist—Mother Nature—our collection highlights materials that blend beauty, durability, and timeless design.

Located in the heart of the Twin Cities' Golden Triangle in Eden Prairie, Gertens Gallery offers a private showroom experience by appointment.

Our team of industry mavens specializes in guiding contractors, designers, and homeowners through the landscape product selection process with expertise and intention.
